MorningFit Privacy Policy

Effective date: 22 November 2026 App: MorningFit (Android package com.binwakili.morningfit, iOS bundle com.binwakili.morningfit) Contact: almustaphawakili@gmail.com

MorningFit is a personal fitness app for a 28-day home workout challenge. It is built to work without an account and to keep your information on your own device.

Summary

  • We do not collect, store, sell or share your personal data. There is no account, no sign-up, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking.
  • Everything you enter stays in a database on your device.
  • The app connects to the internet only to stream exercise demonstration videos from YouTube when you view them.

Information you enter in the app

You may enter: an optional first name; fitness level and push-up ability; goals and available equipment; answers to a health-screening questionnaire and any injuries or limitations you choose to describe; preferred workout time; daily readiness check-ins (energy, sleep, soreness, pain, symptoms); workout results (repetitions, effort ratings, notes).

This information is stored only on your device in the app’s private storage. It is never transmitted to us or to any third party. We, the developer, have no server and cannot see, access or recover it.

How the information is used

Only inside the app on your device: to personalise your workouts (for example, choosing easier or harder exercise versions), schedule reminders, and show your progress.

Exercise videos (YouTube)

Exercise demonstrations are embedded YouTube videos played through YouTube’s privacy-enhanced embed player (youtube-nocookie.com). When a video loads, your device connects directly to YouTube/Google, which receives the technical information any website receives (such as your IP address and device/browser details) and may process it under the Google Privacy Policy. MorningFit does not send any of your workout or profile information to YouTube. You can turn off automatic video loading in Settings → Auto-load demo videos; offline illustrations and written instructions remain available without any connection.

Notifications

Morning reminders are local notifications scheduled on your device. No push-notification service or server is used.

Backups and exports

You can export a backup (JSON) or results (CSV). The file is created on your device and shared only where you choose through your device’s share sheet. You can restore a backup file you select.

Permissions

  • Internet — to stream demonstration videos.
  • Notifications — to show morning reminders (you can decline or turn them off).
  • Vibration — optional haptic cues during workouts.

MorningFit does not access your location, contacts, camera, microphone, photos or files (other than a backup file you explicitly pick).

Data retention and deletion

Your data stays on your device until you delete it. You can erase it at any time in Settings → Erase everything (or reset progress only), or by uninstalling the app. Because we hold no copy, there is nothing for us to delete on our side.

Children

MorningFit is intended for adults and is not directed at children under 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your country). We do not knowingly collect information from children — in fact we collect none from anyone.

Health information disclaimer

MorningFit provides general fitness guidance and is not a medical device. It does not diagnose, treat or prevent any condition. Consult a doctor before starting an exercise program if you have any health concerns.

Security

Data is stored in the app’s private, sandboxed storage protected by your device’s operating system. Keep your device secured with a passcode. Exported backup files are not encrypted; store them somewhere you trust.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published at the same address with a new effective date.
