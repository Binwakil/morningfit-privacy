MorningFit Privacy Policy

Effective date: 25 November 2026 Publisher: Manar Solutions App: MorningFit (Android package com.manar.morningfit, iOS bundle com.manar.morningfit) Contact: manarglobalsolutions@gmail.com

MorningFit is a fitness app for a 28-day home workout challenge, published by Manar Solutions. It works without an account and keeps your workout and health information on your own device.

Summary

  • Your workout, profile and health information never leaves your device. There is no account and no sign-up, and Manar Solutions has no server that receives it.
  • The free version shows ads served by Google AdMob. To deliver and measure ads, Google collects device and usage information (including your device's advertising ID), as described below. Where the law requires it, you are asked for consent first and can change your choice at any time.
  • The app connects to the internet to load ads and to stream exercise demonstration videos from YouTube.

Information you enter in the app

You may enter: an optional first name; fitness level and push-up ability; goals and equipment; answers to a health-screening questionnaire and any injuries or limitations you describe; preferred workout time; daily readiness check-ins (energy, sleep, soreness, pain, symptoms); workout results (repetitions, effort ratings, notes).

This information is stored only on your device in the app's private storage. It is never sent to Manar Solutions, to Google AdMob, or to any other third party, and it is never used to target ads.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

MorningFit uses Google AdMob to show banner ads on browsing screens and, at most about once a day, a full-screen ad after you finish and save a workout. Ads are never shown during onboarding, health screening or a workout.

When an ad is requested, the Google Mobile Ads SDK collects and processes information such as:

  • your device's advertising ID (Android Advertising ID / Apple IDFA where you allow tracking);
  • IP address (used to estimate approximate location), device model, operating system and app version;
  • ad interactions (impressions, clicks) and diagnostics (crash and performance data related to ads).

Google uses this information to deliver ads, measure them, prevent fraud and — where you consent — personalise ads. See How Google uses information from apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

Your choices

  • EEA, UK and Switzerland (and other regions where required): a consent message from Google appears before any ad is loaded. You can change your choice later in Settings → Privacy & ad choices.
  • iPhone: you are asked whether to allow tracking (App Tracking Transparency). If you decline, ads are not personalised with your IDFA.
  • Android: you can reset or delete your advertising ID in your device settings (Settings → Privacy → Ads).

Exercise videos (YouTube)

Exercise demonstrations are YouTube videos played through YouTube's privacy-enhanced embed player (youtube-nocookie.com). When a video loads, your device connects directly to YouTube/Google, which receives the technical information any website receives (such as your IP address and device details). MorningFit does not send any of your workout or profile information to YouTube. You can turn off automatic video loading in Settings → Auto-load demo videos.

Notifications

Morning reminders are local notifications scheduled on your device. No push-notification server is used.

Backups and exports

You can export a backup (JSON) or results (CSV). The file is created on your device and shared only where you choose. You can restore a backup file you select.

Permissions

  • Internet / network state — to load ads and demonstration videos.
  • Advertising ID (Android AD_ID) — used by Google AdMob as described above.
  • Notifications — morning reminders (you can decline or turn them off).
  • Vibration — optional haptic cues during workouts.

MorningFit does not access your location (GPS), contacts, camera, microphone, photos or files (other than a backup file you explicitly pick).

Data retention and deletion

Your workout data stays on your device until you delete it: Settings → Erase everything, or uninstall the app. Manar Solutions holds no copy. Data collected by Google for advertising is retained under Google's policies; you can reset your advertising ID at any time.

Children

MorningFit is intended for adults (18+) and is not directed at children. Ads are requested as general-audience ads and the app does not knowingly collect information from children.

Health information disclaimer

MorningFit provides general fitness guidance and is not a medical device. It does not diagnose, treat or prevent any condition. Consult a doctor before starting an exercise program if you have any health concerns.

Security

Workout data is stored in the app's private, sandboxed storage protected by your device's operating system. Exported backup files are not encrypted; store them somewhere you trust. Ad requests are sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS).
